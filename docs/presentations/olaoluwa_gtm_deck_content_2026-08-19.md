## Slide 2 — Reconciliation is a productivity and governance problem

**Productivity pressure**

Finance and operations teams reconcile payment, settlement, and ledger records across disparate rails, portals, and spreadsheets. Exceptions consume scarce capacity and delay close.

**Governance exposure**

Unresolved breaks make it harder to see financial exposure, assign accountable ownership, and assemble audit-ready evidence for resolution.

**The market gap**

Many institutions own tools, yet still rely on manual work where those tools are expensive, insufficiently tailored, or disconnected from the operational workflow.

> The sale is not “AI matching.” It is faster financial certainty with stronger operational control.

## Slide 3 — ReconcileAI is the control layer between payment events and financial certainty

**1. Ingest & normalise**

Bring together transaction, payment, settlement, and ledger records across institutional and commerce channels.

**2. Match & explain**

Use deterministic matching with ML-assisted classification to identify, prioritise, and explain exceptions.

**3. Govern & resolve**

Route work through a human-in-the-loop review queue with accountable actions and an immutable audit trail.

**Deployment principle:** On-premise for regulated financial institutions; SaaS or managed deployment for FMCG and corporate B2B.

## Slide 4 — Four ICPs, one recurring operational-control job

| ICP | Initial buyer group | Primary trigger | Deployment route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial banks | COO, CIO, Financial Control, Operations | Payment/ledger breaks, close pressure, control evidence | On-premise |
| Microfinance banks | COO, Operations, Finance | Lean teams scaling payment operations | On-premise or private managed |
| Fintechs | COO, Head of Operations, Finance | Settlement assurance across growing payment flows | Private managed or on-premise |
| Corporate B2B / FMCG | Financial Controller, Finance Operations | Distributor receipts across bank and mobile-money rails | SaaS |

**Initial wedge:** Nigerian financial services active pipeline; Uganda FMCG distributors as the corporate-B2B beachhead.

**Qualification test:** A compelling first meeting requires a visible break, a named process owner, a material close/control consequence, and a credible technical deployment path.

## Slide 5 — Payment rails are scaling faster than control operations

**US$2.1 trillion**

Mobile-money transaction value in 2025 globally. [GSMA, 2026]

**US$155 billion**

Mobile-money merchant payments in 2025 globally, almost 50% growth year on year. [GSMA, 2026]

BCG projection for African fintech revenues by 2030, led by digital payments. [BCG, 2026]

**The GTM implication:** Prioritise institutions where payment scale, fragmented data, and governance accountability meet. Build the pipeline through discovery-led meetings; capture buyer signals, map the control owner, and help the founder convert pain into a paid POC.
