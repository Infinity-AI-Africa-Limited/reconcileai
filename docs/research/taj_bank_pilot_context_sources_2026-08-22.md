# Taj Bank Pilot Context — Source Notes

**Date:** 22 August 2026  
**Purpose:** Keep the Taj Bank pilot plan evidence-based. Public sources establish only outward-facing digital-channel context; they do **not** establish Taj Bank’s core-banking vendor, network topology, data-classification policy, cloud policy, identity provider, key-management standard, or approval workflow.

## Verified Public Context

TAJBank publicly describes itself as a CBN-licensed digital bank, with customer access through branches, its TAJWay mobile application, USSD `*898#`, and internet banking. Its public material also describes retail and business banking offerings. [1] [2]

This supports a **multi-channel reconciliation discovery hypothesis**, not an implementation claim. ReconcileAI must obtain the actual authoritative data-source inventory and control requirements from Taj Bank before it selects the first reconciliation use case.

## Planning Consequence

The initial proposal is a **read-only, parallel pilot** in a Taj Bank-controlled non-production or private environment. It will not write to a core-banking system, payment switch, general ledger, customer account, or production workflow. The pilot data scope, masking standard, secure-transfer mechanism, environment, and acceptance metrics remain Taj Bank decisions.
