The narration is presented in six visual chapters, using browser-native captures from the authenticated production platform. No generated or recreated platform imagery is permitted.

| Narration section | Platform visual | On-screen purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening and disclosure | Financial Services dashboard | Establish the live platform and visible controlled-data disclosure. |
