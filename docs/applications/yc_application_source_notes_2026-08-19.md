# YC Application — Source Notes

## Official YC guidance

| Source | Application implication | URL |
| --- | --- | --- |
| YC, *How to Apply to Y Combinator* | Lead with a matter-of-fact description; be exceptionally clear, concise, specific, and free of inflated marketing language. Use specific founder achievements and disclose real constraints rather than obscuring them. | https://www.ycombinator.com/howtoapply |
| YC FAQ | YC funds companies at very early stages, including pre-revenue companies, and expects founders to commit full-time during and after the batch if accepted. | https://www.ycombinator.com/faq |
| YC, *The Application Video* | The one-minute founder video should contain only founders talking, introduce the founders and what they are making and why, and should be spoken from bullets rather than read from a script. | https://www.ycombinator.com/video |
| YC, *Apply to Y Combinator* | The Fall 2026 batch runs October–December in San Francisco; late applications remain under review though no decision date is guaranteed. | https://www.ycombinator.com/apply |

## Competitor validation

SmartStream and AutoRek are appropriate enterprise-comparison examples: their official product pages describe automated, enterprise reconciliation for banks, payments, and other financial institutions. The YC answer should not dismiss them; it should explain ReconcileAI’s initial wedge as African payment operations, deployment flexibility, and governed exception workflow.
