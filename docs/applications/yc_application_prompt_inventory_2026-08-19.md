# YC Application — Prompt Inventory

Source: supplied screenshot of the YC application form, viewed on 19 August 2026.

## Founders

1. Who writes code, or does other technical work on the product? Was any of it done by a non-founder? Please explain.
2. Are you looking for a cofounder?
3. Founder video upload.

## Company

1. Company name.
2. Describe what your company does in 50 characters or less.
3. Company URL, if any.
4. Product demo upload, if available.
5. Product link, if any.
6. What is your company going to make? Please describe your product and what it does or will do.
7. Where do you live now, and where would the company be based after YC?
8. Explain your decision regarding location.

## Progress

1. How far along are you?
2. How long have each of you been working on this? How much of that has been full-time?
3. What tech stack are you using, or planning to use, to build this product? Include all models and AI coding tools you use.
4. Are people using your product?
5. Do you have revenue?
6. If you previously applied with the same idea, what changed? If you applied with a different idea, why did you pivot and what did you learn from the earlier idea?
7. If you have already participated in or committed to participate in an incubator, "accelerator" or "pre-accelerator" program, please tell us about it.

## Idea

1. Why did you pick this idea to work on? Do you have domain expertise in this area? How do you know people need what you are making?
2. Who are your competitors? What do you understand about your business that they do not?
3. How do or will you make money? How much could you make?
4. If you had any other ideas you considered applying with, please list them.

## Equity

1. Have you formed ANY legal entity yet?
2. Have you taken any investment yet?
3. Are you currently fundraising?

## Curious

1. What convinced you to apply to Y Combinator? Did someone encourage you to apply? Have you been to any YC events?
2. How did you hear about Y Combinator?

## Batch preference

The form shown offers Fall 2026 and a default Fall 2026 option.

## Extraction caveat

The supplied screenshot is a tall image-only PDF. The prompts above are the verified readable labels and questions; no character limits other than the 50-character company description were legible in the supplied form.
